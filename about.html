About FEWA

Where beauty meets skill and opportunity

FEWA Beauty & Fashion College develops skilled, creative and confident professionals through practical training in beauty, fashion and personal care.

Our Mission

We exist to train, inspire, and launch beauty and fashion professionals — grounded in faith, driven by passion, and equipped to lead with skill and style.

Our Vision

To be a beacon of faith-driven creativity, where every student discovers their God-given craft, radiates confidence, and rises to shape the future of beauty and fashion — one purpose-filled design at a time.

Our Motto

Craft It. Glow In It. Own Your Style

Core Values

  • God-Centeredness
  • Teamwork
  • Integrity
  • Professionalism
  • Discipline

Our approach

Students learn from experienced trainers in a hands-on environment designed to build technical ability, confidence and career readiness.

Campus facilities

Our practical learning spaces support salon practice, beauty therapy, garment construction and creative styling.
