Our Approach to Learning

At FEWA, learning goes beyond theory. Students develop their technical abilities, creativity and confidence through practical training and guided learning, with an emphasis on building the skills needed to pursue opportunities in the beauty and fashion industries.

Our Campus Facilities

Our learning spaces support practical activities in beauty therapy, salon practice, garment construction and creative styling. These spaces are intended to help students develop their skills and translate creative ideas into practical work.
