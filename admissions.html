Admissions support

Frequently Asked Questions

I don't have all my documents yet -- can I still start?

Yes. You can start your application, fill in your details, and use Save & Continue Later to come back once you have everything. Your progress is kept securely.

I forgot my application password -- what do I do?

On the application page, choose Continue Existing Application and use the Forgot password? link to reset it by email.

How do I check my application status?

Use Already submitted? Check Application Status on the application page with your reference number and the email or phone you applied with. See After Submission above.

What are the admission requirements?

Requirements depend on the qualification level you're entering at -- from KCPE/KCSE (any grade) for Trade Test and Level 3, up to a KCSE C- for Diploma level. Contact FEWA Admissions to confirm the exact requirement for your chosen programme.

When is the next intake?

FEWA admits students in January, May, and September intakes each year. You can start your online application today.

Can I study part-time?

Yes, flexible full-time weekday classes and part-time evening/weekend modules are available to accommodate working students. You'll choose your study mode in Step 3 of the application.

Who can I contact if I'm stuck?

Our admissions team is happy to help at any stage -- see Need Help? below.
